# AI Worker Integration

> **Deploy AI workers across business analysis, architecture, development, QA, DevOps, and customer success.**

---

## Description

AI Worker Integration activates Arjun Automation's pre-built AI agents within your operating environment. Unlike generic chatbots, our AI workers execute structured workflows — drafting requirements, generating solution designs, producing code, running test suites, orchestrating deployments, and monitoring customer health — all with audit trails and human approval gates.

This service configures, trains, and integrates AI workers into your delivery and operations pipelines.

---

## Problems Solved

- **Delivery bottlenecks** — BA and architect capacity limiting project throughput
- **Inconsistent quality** — Ad hoc AI tool usage without governance or standards
- **No audit trail** — AI-generated outputs not tracked or reviewable
- **Siloed AI experiments** — Teams using different tools with no shared context
- **Slow customer success** — Manual health scoring and reactive support

## Deliverables

1. AI worker activation for selected roles (BA, Architect, Dev, QA, DevOps, CS)
2. Workflow integration — AI workers embedded in project and delivery pipelines
3. Knowledge base configuration feeding AI context (org policies, templates, standards)
4. Approval gates and human-in-the-loop rules per worker type
5. Audit log configuration for all AI-generated outputs
6. Team training on AI worker interaction and review workflows
7. 30-day tuning period with performance review
8. AI operations runbook
