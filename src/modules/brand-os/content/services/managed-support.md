# Managed Support

> **Ongoing platform operations, monitoring, updates, and customer success.**

---

## Description

Managed Support keeps your Arjun Automation OS deployment healthy, secure, and evolving after go-live. Our operations team provides 24/7 platform monitoring, proactive maintenance, security patching, performance optimization, user support escalation, and quarterly business reviews — so your internal team focuses on business operations, not platform administration.

---

## Problems Solved

- **Post-go-live neglect** — Platform deployed but no one maintaining or optimizing it
- **Internal ops burden** — IT team pulled into platform admin instead of strategic work
- **Slow issue resolution** — No dedicated support channel with SLA guarantees
- **Missed updates** — Security patches and feature releases not applied timely
- **No success measurement** — Platform live but adoption and ROI not tracked

---

## Deliverables

1. 24/7 platform health monitoring and alerting
2. Dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM)
3. Tiered support desk with SLA-backed response times
4. Monthly platform health report
5. Quarterly business review (QBR) with adoption metrics and recommendations
6. Security patch and platform update management
7. User provisioning and access management support
8. Backup verification and disaster recovery testing (quarterly)
9. Feature release notes and upgrade guidance
10. Priority escalation channel for P1 incidents
