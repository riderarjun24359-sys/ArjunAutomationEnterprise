# Enterprise Consulting

> **Strategic advisory for digital transformation, operating model design, and automation roadmap.**

---

## Description

Enterprise Consulting provides strategic advisory before, during, or independent of platform deployment. Our consultants — experienced operators and architects — help you assess current-state operations, design target operating models, build automation roadmaps, evaluate build-vs-platform decisions, and prepare your organization for transformation.

This is ideal for organizations in the planning phase or those needing expert guidance without immediate platform commitment.

---

## Problems Solved

- **Unclear transformation path** — Leadership knows they need to modernize but lacks a concrete plan
- **Build vs. buy paralysis** — Engineering wants to build; operations needs speed
- **Siloed initiatives** — Departmental automation projects without enterprise coherence
- **ROI uncertainty** — No framework for measuring automation investment returns
- **Change management gaps** — Technology selected without organizational readiness plan

---

## Deliverables

1. Current-state assessment report (processes, systems, pain points)
2. Target operating model design document
3. Automation opportunity map with prioritized initiatives
4. Build vs. platform recommendation with TCO analysis
5. 12–24 month transformation roadmap with milestones
6. Change management and training plan outline
7. Executive presentation (board-ready)
8. Optional: RFP/evaluation framework if assessing vendors

---

## Benefits

- **Clarity before commitment** — Make informed platform and investment decisions
- **Executive alignment** — Shared vision document for leadership and board
- **Risk reduction** — Identify integration, data, and change risks early
- **Accelerated procurement** — Ready-to-use evaluation criteria and business case
- **Independent expertise** — Vendor-neutral advisory (platform recommendation only if requested)
