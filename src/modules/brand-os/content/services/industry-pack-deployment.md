# Industry Pack Deployment

> **Pre-built industry operating systems for rapid vertical go-live.**

---

## Description

Industry Pack Deployment accelerates go-live by deploying a pre-configured industry operating system — Restaurant OS, Hospital OS, School OS, or Retail OS — on top of Arjun Automation OS. Each pack includes industry-specific workflows, forms, documents, dashboards, and role configurations validated across multiple deployments.

We customize the pack to your locations, branding, and specific operational requirements while preserving the proven foundation.

---

## Problems Solved

- **Long vertical software evaluations** — Months comparing niche vendors with limited integration
- **Custom build risk** — Expensive, slow development for standard industry workflows
- **Multi-location inconsistency** — Each site running different processes and tools
- **Compliance gaps** — Industry-specific audit and documentation requirements unmet
- **Delayed time-to-revenue** — New locations or facilities waiting on operational systems

## Deliverables

1. Selected Industry OS pack deployed and configured
2. Multi-location / multi-facility hierarchy setup
3. Industry workflows, forms, and document templates activated
4. Role-based access for industry-specific personas (e.g., chef, nurse, teacher, store manager)
5. Industry dashboard and KPI configuration
6. Integration with POS, EMR, SIS, or inventory systems (1–2 integrations)
7. Location-specific branding and menu/catalog configuration
8. Industry admin and operator training
9. Go-live support and 30-day hypercare
