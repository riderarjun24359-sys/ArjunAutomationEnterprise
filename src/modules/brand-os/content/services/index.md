## How to Use This Library

1. **Discovery** — Use the Discovery Questionnaire to identify the right service mix
2. **Proposal** — Pull scope language from individual service pages into the Proposal Template
3. **Pricing** — Reference Pricing Structure sections for quotation building
4. **Delivery** — Hand off to delivery teams with the Statement of Work template

---

## Engagement Models

- **Fixed Scope** — Defined deliverables, delivery roadmap, and price (recommended for Platform Implementation and Industry Packs)
- **Time & Materials** — Consulting engagements with weekly caps and milestone reviews
- **Retainer** — Managed Support with SLA tiers (Standard, Premium, Enterprise)
