# Platform Implementation

> **Full Arjun Automation OS deployment, configuration, and integration.**

---

## Description

Platform Implementation is our flagship engagement — deploying the complete Arjun Automation OS for your organization. We provision your tenant, configure core modules (CRM, Projects, Finance, HR, Tasks, Documents), set up enterprise identity and governance, integrate with your existing systems, and train your team for go-live.

This service transforms fragmented tools and manual processes into a unified, AI-ready operating system tailored to your workflows.

---

## Problems Solved

- **Tool sprawl** — Dozens of disconnected SaaS subscriptions with no shared data
- **Manual handoffs** — Email and spreadsheet-driven processes between departments
- **No audit trail** — Compliance and governance gaps across business operations
- **Slow internal builds** — Engineering teams maintaining custom tools instead of product work
- **AI bolt-on fatigue** — Chatbots and copilots that don't integrate with actual workflows

---

## Deliverables

1. Provisioned Arjun Automation OS tenant with enterprise identity (MFA, RBAC)
2. Configured core modules: CRM, Projects, Tasks, Finance, HR, Inventory, Documents
3. Custom workflow and form configurations per agreed scope
4. Integration with up to 3 external systems (ERP, accounting, communication)
5. Data migration from legacy systems (up to agreed record volumes)
6. Admin and end-user training (virtual, up to 40 hours)
7. UAT support and go-live cutover
8. 30-day hypercare post-launch
9. Go-live documentation and runbook
