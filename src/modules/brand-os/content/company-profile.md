## About Us

Arjun Automation is an enterprise automation company headquartered in India, serving mid-market and enterprise clients globally. We build and operate **Arjun Automation OS** — a unified business operating system that combines CRM, project management, finance, HR, inventory, AI workers, industry-specific modules, and enterprise governance into one secure, composable platform.

Founded by operators who lived through painful digital transformation programs, we exist to eliminate the false choice between speed and enterprise rigor. Our clients launch new operations in weeks, not years — with the security, auditability, and scalability their boards demand.
