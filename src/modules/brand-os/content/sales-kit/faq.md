# Sales FAQ

> **Frequently asked questions for prospects, partners, and sales conversations.**

---

## Platform & Product

**Q: What is Arjun Automation OS?**  
A: An enterprise business operating system with 40+ integrated modules — CRM, projects, finance, HR, AI workers, industry packs, and governance — in one secure platform.

**Q: Is this a SaaS product or a custom build?**  
A: SaaS platform with configuration and customization. We don't write bespoke code from scratch — we configure proven modules and workflows to your needs.

**Q: How is this different from Salesforce, Monday, or Zoho?**  
A: Those are point solutions. Arjun Automation OS is a unified operating system — one data layer, one identity system, AI workers, industry packs, and enterprise governance built in.

**Q: Can we start with a few modules and expand?**  
A: Yes. Most clients start with CRM, Projects, Tasks, and Finance, then add modules over time. The platform is designed for progressive adoption.

**Q: Do you offer a trial or demo environment?**  
A: Yes. We provide guided demos and can provision a demo tenant for qualified prospects during evaluation.

## AI & Automation

**Q: What are AI workers?**  
A: Pre-built AI agents for Business Analysis, Solution Architecture, Development, QA, DevOps, and Customer Success. They execute workflows — not just chat.

**Q: Is our data used to train public AI models?**  
A: No. Your data stays in your tenant with RLS. AI workers operate within your organization's context only.

**Q: Can we control what AI workers do?**  
A: Yes. Approval gates, audit logs, and human-in-the-loop rules are configurable per worker type.

**Q: How is data secured?**

**Q: Can we deploy in our own cloud?**  
A: Enterprise tier supports dedicated deployment options. Contact sales for details.

**Q: Where is data stored?**
