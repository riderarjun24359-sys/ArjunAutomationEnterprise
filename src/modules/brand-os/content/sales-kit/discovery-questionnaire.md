# Discovery Questionnaire

> **Pre-engagement discovery for sales and solution architecture teams.**

---

## Section A: Organization

1. **Company name and industry?**
2. **Number of employees? Locations/facilities?**
3. **Annual revenue range?** (helps scope appropriately)
4. **Who is the executive sponsor for this initiative?**
5. **What triggered this evaluation now?** (event, pain point, mandate)

---

## Section B: Current State

6. **What systems do you use today for:**
   - CRM / customer management
   - Project / task management
   - Finance / accounting
   - HR / payroll
   - Industry-specific ops (POS, EMR, SIS, etc.)
7. **What works well in your current setup?**
8. **Top 3 operational pain points?**
9. **How are reports and dashboards produced today?**
10. **Any compliance or regulatory requirements?** (HIPAA, PCI, GDPR, etc.)

---

## Section C: Desired Outcomes

11. **What does success look like in 90 days? In 12 months?**
12. **Which departments must be live at go-live?**
13. **Must-have vs. nice-to-have features?**
14. **Are you evaluating build vs. buy? What's your lean?**
15. **Any existing AI or automation initiatives?**

---

## Section D: Technical Environment

16. **Cloud preference?** (Our platform, client cloud, hybrid)
17. **Identity provider?** (Google Workspace, Azure AD, Okta, other)
18. **Systems requiring integration?** (List with API availability)
19. **Data volumes for migration?** (Users, records, transactions)
20. **Existing data quality concerns?**

---

## Section E: Team & Change

21. **Who will be the day-to-day project contact?**
22. **Hours/week the client team can dedicate?**
23. **Previous platform migration experience?**
24. **Change management resources available?**
25. **Training preferences?** (Virtual, on-site, self-paced)

---

## Section F: Commercial

26. **Budget range for this initiative?**
27. **Target go-live date?** (Hard deadline or flexible)
28. **Decision timeline?** (When do you need to choose a vendor)
29. **Other vendors being evaluated?**
30. **Managed support interest post-go-live?**

---

## Scoring Guide (Internal)

| Signal | Recommended Service |
|--------|---------------------|
| Needs full business ops platform | Platform Implementation |
| Vertical-specific (restaurant, hospital, etc.) | Industry Pack Deployment |
| Has platform, wants AI acceleration | AI Worker Integration |
| Pre-decision, needs strategy | Enterprise Consulting |
| Post-go-live, needs ongoing ops | Managed Support |
