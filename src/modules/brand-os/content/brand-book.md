## Company Story

Arjun Automation was founded on a simple conviction: every organization deserves enterprise-grade automation without enterprise-grade complexity. Born from years of delivering large-scale digital transformation programs, we saw the same pattern repeat — fragmented tools, manual handoffs, and AI bolted on as an afterthought rather than woven into the operating fabric.

We built Arjun Automation OS to change that. Our platform unifies CRM, projects, finance, AI workers, industry operating systems, and governance into a single composable stack. From restaurant chains to hospitals, schools to retail networks, we help organizations launch faster, operate smarter, and scale with confidence.

Today, Arjun Automation serves forward-thinking enterprises and growth-stage companies who refuse to choose between speed and rigor. We are builders, operators, and partners — not just vendors.

## Tone of Voice

### We Sound Like
- A senior operator who has done this before
- A technical partner who explains without jargon
- An advisor who respects the client's intelligence

### Voice Attributes
- **Clear** — Short sentences, active voice, defined terms
- **Assured** — State capabilities confidently; avoid hedging ("might," "possibly")
- **Human** — Warm but professional; never robotic or overly casual
- **Specific** — Use numbers, timelines, and concrete examples

### Example
❌ *"Our solution leverages synergistic AI capabilities to potentially optimize workflows."*
✅ *"Deploy AI workers across your delivery pipeline in two weeks. BA agents draft requirements; Dev Factory generates code; QA agents run test suites — all auditable."*

---

## Writing Guidelines

1. **Lead with the outcome** — Start with what the reader gains, not what we built.
2. **Use the client's language** — Mirror industry terms (patient, guest, student) not internal codenames.
3. **One idea per paragraph** — Especially in proposals and sales collateral.
4. **Quantify when possible** — "40+ modules," "90-day go-live," "RLS on every table."
5. **Avoid acronyms on first use** — Spell out, then abbreviate: "Row Level Security (RLS)."
6. **Active voice** — "We deploy" not "Deployment is performed."
7. **Consistent naming** — Always "Arjun Automation" (not "Arjun," "AA," or "the platform" alone in formal docs).
8. **PDF formatting** — Use H2 for major sections, tables for comparisons, horizontal rules sparingly.

## Brand Don'ts

- ❌ Don't promise custom features outside the platform roadmap without scoping
- ❌ Don't use "AI magic" or vague automation claims without specifics
- ❌ Don't compare directly to consumer apps (Slack, Notion) — we are enterprise OS
- ❌ Don't dilute the brand with off-palette colors (avoid pink/green themes in Brand OS materials)
- ❌ Don't share client data, logos, or case details without written approval
- ❌ Don't undersell security — never describe RLS or audit as "optional extras"
- ❌ Don't use internal codenames (AAGE, AGO, etc.) in client-facing brand materials
